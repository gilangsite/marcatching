## Scoring Guide

### 9–10
Excellent. Feels production-ready.

### 8
Strong. Usable with small refinement.

### 7
Decent, but not stable enough for final engine quality.

### 6 or below
Weak. Needs revision.

---

## Pass / Fail Decision

### Pass
Overall score is 80% or above and no critical failure exists.

### Revise
Overall score is 70–79% or one important category feels weak.

### Fail
Overall score is below 70% or the output fails to feel like Marcatching.

---

## Critical Failure Checklist

Even if the total score looks acceptable, mark the test as revise or fail if any of these happen:

- hook is weak or generic
- content score is too lenient
- copy does not feel Marcatching
- design brief is visually generic
- output skips mandatory workflow order
- final output is messy or hard to execute
- source-sensitive claims are made without research
