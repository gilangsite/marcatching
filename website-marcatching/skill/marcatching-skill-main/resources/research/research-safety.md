# Marcatching Research Safety

This skill protects Marcatching output from invented facts, weak sources, and misleading simplification.

Use it for:

- medical content
- health education
- finance/legal/tax content
- trend research
- company/brand facts
- market data
- statistics
- current news
- product claims
- competitor research
- educational content with factual risk

## Source Priority

Use sources in this order:

1. Official website or primary source
2. Official documentation or guideline
3. Peer-reviewed paper or recognized institution
4. Government or professional body
5. Reputable news or industry publication
6. User-provided internal source
7. Low-authority sources only as non-decisive context

## Never

- invent statistics
- cite random blogs as proof
- turn correlation into causation
- remove uncertainty
- write medical advice as diagnosis
- claim official data without source
- use old data as current

## If Browsing Is Available

Research before writing.

Cite or mention source type where appropriate.

## If Browsing Is Not Available

Ask user for sources or write:

```text
Draft ini membutuhkan verifikasi sumber sebelum dipublikasikan.
```

## Research Output Format

```text
Research question:
Source needed:
Available evidence:
Confidence level:
Claims allowed:
Claims to avoid:
Safe framing:
Recommended wording:
```

## Medical Safety

For medical content:

```text
Do not diagnose.
Do not prescribe.
Add educational boundary when needed.
Use credible medical references.
Flag claims that require clinician review.
```
