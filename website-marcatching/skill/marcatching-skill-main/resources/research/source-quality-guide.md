# Source Quality Guide

## Strong Sources

- Official brand/product website
- Product documentation
- Government health agencies
- Professional medical associations
- Peer-reviewed journals
- Recognized textbooks/guidelines
- Official company reports
- Regulatory filings
- High-quality industry reports

## Weak Sources

- random SEO blogs
- anonymous posts
- unsourced infographics
- viral social posts
- outdated articles
- copied summaries without source
- AI-generated articles

## Confidence Labels

```text
High confidence: supported by primary or authoritative sources.
Medium confidence: supported by reputable secondary sources.
Low confidence: based on user-provided unsourced material or general reasoning.
Needs verification: do not publish as fact yet.
```
