# Content Creation Preflight

Use this before writing any content.

The purpose is to prevent the model from jumping directly into generic content.

## Preflight Questions

```text
1. What platform is this for?
2. What format is expected?
3. Who is the audience?
4. What is their current emotional state?
5. What is the content goal?
6. What should the audience do after consuming it?
7. What makes this content worth watching or reading?
8. What makes this content different from common content on the same topic?
9. Does this need trend research?
10. Does this need factual/source validation?
11. Does this need brand learning?
12. Which content role should handle this?
```

## Required Strategy Brief

Before writing final content, produce internally:

```text
Content Role:
Platform:
Audience:
Audience Trigger:
Content Tension:
Angle:
Hook Hypothesis:
Retention Device:
Format:
CTA:
Risk:
```

## No-Direct-Generation Rule

Do not produce final content until the strategy brief is clear.

If the brief is weak, improve the angle before writing.

## Content Creation Roles

- Strategy, pillars, roadmap → Content Intelligence Strategist
- Trends, competitor, FYP research → Viral Content Researcher
- Ideas, angles, series → Viral Idea Architect
- Scripts, storytelling, carousel → Storytelling and Scriptwriting Engine
- Hook, retention, first 3 seconds → Hook and Retention Engineer
- Medical/factual/sensitive review → Content Doctor and Fact Safety Reviewer
- FYP/flop/performance audit → FYP Analyst and Content Performance Doctor
- Calendar, repurposing, SOP → Repurposing and Production System Planner
