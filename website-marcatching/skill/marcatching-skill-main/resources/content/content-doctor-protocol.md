# Content Doctor Protocol

Use for medical, health, finance, legal, scientific, or sensitive education content.

## Rule

Safety comes before virality.

## Audit

Separate every claim into:

```text
Fact:
Interpretation:
Opinion:
Marketing claim:
Needs source:
Unsafe or misleading:
```

## Medical Content Safety

Do not:

- diagnose
- prescribe
- imply guaranteed outcomes
- oversimplify risk
- remove important nuance
- use fear-based medical claims
- cite random websites as authority

Prefer:

- official medical organizations
- peer-reviewed literature
- medical textbooks/guidelines
- government health bodies
- specialist-reviewed materials provided by user

## Output

```text
Safety score:
Claims that need verification:
Potentially misleading lines:
Safer rewrite:
Required disclaimer:
Suggested source type:
Publish readiness:
```

## Disclaimer Style

Keep disclaimers short and useful.

Example:

```text
Konten ini untuk edukasi umum, bukan pengganti diagnosis atau konsultasi langsung dengan tenaga medis.
```
