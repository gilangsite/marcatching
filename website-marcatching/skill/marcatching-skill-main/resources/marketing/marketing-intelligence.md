# Marcatching Marketing Intelligence

This skill creates marketing outputs that feel specific, bold, grounded, and psychologically sharp.

Use this skill for:

- copywriting
- landing page copy
- ads copy
- offer framing
- funnel strategy
- campaign messaging
- emotional prompt library execution
- caption and carousel copy
- product positioning
- brand messaging
- sales page sections

## Required Behavior

Before writing:

1. Understand the product.
2. Identify the audience's psychological state.
3. Identify the unique mechanism or differentiator.
4. Check proof and source quality.
5. Choose the emotional strategy.
6. Match the funnel stage.
7. Write.
8. Score and rewrite.

## Brand Learning Rule

If the brand/product is public and browsing is available:

1. Search the official website first.
2. Use official social media or product pages next.
3. Use reputable third-party sources only as support.
4. Do not rely on random blogs for core brand facts.

If browsing is not available, ask the user for:

- website link
- product page
- brand guide
- example posts
- offer details
- proof/testimonials

## Output Standard

Marketing output must not sound like:

```text
Di era digital saat ini...
Solusi terbaik...
Maksimalkan potensi...
Yuk buruan...
Jangan sampai ketinggalan...
```

Marketing output should feel:

```text
clear
specific
sharp
not lebay
not pasaran
based on product truth
based on audience psychology
```

## Final Check

Before final output, use `marcatching-evaluator` or the Marketing Quality Gate:

```text
Audience Fit:
Product Specificity:
Differentiation:
Emotional Precision:
Proof Safety:
Copy Sharpness:
Cognitive Ease:

```

If any score is below 8, rewrite.
