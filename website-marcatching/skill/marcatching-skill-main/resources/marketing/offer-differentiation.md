# Offer Differentiation Protocol

Use this when output feels generic or similar to competitors.

## Questions

```text
What is the unique mechanism?
What is the audience's current alternative?
What does the product do differently?
What proof supports the difference?
What pain does the competitor ignore?
What belief does the offer challenge?
```
