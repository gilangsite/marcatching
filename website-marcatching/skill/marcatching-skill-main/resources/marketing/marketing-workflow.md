# Marketing Intelligence Workflow

## Step 1: Brand/Product Intake

Capture:

```text
What is being sold?
Who is it for?
What problem does it solve?
What proof exists?
What makes it different?
What is the desired action?
What channel is this for?
```

## Step 2: Audience OS

Map:

```text
Pain:
Desire:
Fear:
Status:
Friction:
Trigger:
```

## Step 3: Product Truth

Find the non-generic product truth:

```text
Feature:
Benefit:
Mechanism:
Proof:
Difference:
Limitation:
```

If there is no proof, do not invent it.

## Step 4: Emotional Strategy

Choose 1 primary and up to 2 supporting emotional levers.

Example:

```text
Primary: Trust
Secondary: Relief
Support: Premium Perception
```

## Step 5: Funnel Fit

Choose stage:

```text
Awareness: make them notice
Interest: make them understand
Consideration: make them trust
Conversion: make action clear
Retention: make them feel progress
Referral: make sharing feel valuable
```

## Step 6: Write

Write in the requested format.

## Step 7: Evaluate

Score. Rewrite if below 8.
