# Emotional Levers

## Trust
Reduce perceived risk with proof, specificity, process, and clarity.

## Urgency
Make timing matter without fake scarcity.

## Premium Perception
Increase perceived value with restraint, specificity, taste, and identity.

## Identity Signaling
Make the action feel like a reflection of who the audience wants to become.

## Loss Aversion
Show the cost of staying the same without fearmongering.

## Cognitive Ease
Make the message easier to understand and remember.

## Belonging
Make the audience feel seen and represented.

## Relief
Make the problem feel lighter and more actionable.
