# Marcatching Homepage Motion Concept

## Direction

Motion must explain how Marcatching turns information into business outcomes. It is not ambient decoration. Text, navigation, and calls to action stay stable while one visual story carries each section.

## Motion system

- One primary motion graphic per section.
- Slow, synchronized movement with a clear beginning and end.
- No global particle field, custom cursor, page preloader, floating section indicator, or continuous scroll decoration.
- Hover motion is limited to interactive cards and buttons.
- All continuous animation stops when reduced motion is requested.

## Section concepts

1. **Hero — Content engine**
   `Idea → AI → Content / Product → Money`. A single signal moves through the pipeline. The visual is horizontal on desktop and vertical on mobile.
2. **The problem — Noise to signal**
   A small set of scattered inputs sits beside one structured decision flow. The contrast explains why more content is not the answer.
3. **AI adoption gap — Operational bridge**
   A single pulse travels through the implementation steps, connecting AI hype to repeatable workflows and outcomes.
4. **Data urgency — Evidence windows**
   Each data visualization appears with its chapter and then remains stable. Charts do not loop continuously.
5. **What we build — System previews**
   Product-like mini interfaces remain still until the user hovers or opens a module.
6. **Ecosystem — Insight to impact**
   A readable flow connects education, validation, tools, implementation, and impact. Cards use only restrained hover feedback.
7. **Impact — Count once**
   Metrics count up once when they enter the viewport. Supporting bars remain stable afterward.
8. **Pillars — One behavior per principle**
   Noise resolves into signal, human and machine converge, perception gains context, and evidence is verified. Each graphic has at most one slow moving element.
9. **Founder and audience paths**
   Content-led sections use only a short reveal. No decorative loop competes with the copy.
10. **Finale — Quiet system halo**
    One slow halo reinforces completion behind a stable final call to action.

## Responsive rules

- Typography uses bounded `clamp()` sizes and never relies on oversized viewport units alone.
- Motion graphics are width-constrained and use intrinsic ratios.
- Desktop flows become vertical or wrapped flows below 760px.
- Touch targets stay at least 44px, text remains at least 14px for secondary labels, and no essential content requires horizontal page scrolling.
- Mobile sections use content-driven height rather than forced full-screen layouts.
